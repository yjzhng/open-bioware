Blocked the first time you open it?

OmicExplorer is not signed by Apple or Microsoft, so your computer may block it at first. To open it:

macOS

  • macOS 15 or newer: Double-click the app. When it says it can't be opened, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  • macOS 14 or older: Right-click the app, choose Open, then click Open again.

Or run this in Terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/OmicExplorer.app

Windows

  • "Windows protected your PC": Click More info, then Run anyway.
  • File blocked: Right-click the .exe, choose Properties, tick Unblock at the bottom, click OK, then run it.

Overview

Load your own data, or turn one raw export from DIA-NN, Spectronaut or MaxQuant into ready-to-use files with the guided import. Annotations such as GO, KEGG, Reactome and STRING are fetched for you.

Build the analysis as a workflow of steps: clean data, compare, contrast and curve fit. Save it all in one project file. Results open in a dashboard of linked plots, where hovering or pinning a gene highlights it everywhere. Export plots as SVG, PNG or PDF and tables as CSV or XLSX.

Features

  • Workflow editor

    Chain steps such as Load data, Clean data and Compare on a canvas. Save the whole analysis in one project file.

  • Guided import

    Turn a raw DIA-NN, Spectronaut or MaxQuant export into data, samplesheet and metadata files. Annotations are fetched for you.

  • Built-in statistics

    Filtering, imputation, normalisation and batch correction, then t-tests, ANOVA, contrasts and dose or time curve fits.

  • Linked plots

    Volcano, heatmap, PCA, enrichment, STRING network and more. Hover or pin a gene to highlight it in every plot.

  • Local and private

    Runs on your computer with no Python, server or account. Your data never leaves your machine.

  • Editable exports

    Plots as SVG, PNG or PDF, with shapes that stay editable in Illustrator. Tables as CSV or XLSX.
